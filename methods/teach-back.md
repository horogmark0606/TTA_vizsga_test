# Teach-back
**When:** after a `mentor` session on a topic, or when answers feel memorized but shallow.
**Steps:** 1) Student picks a concept. 2) Agent plays a curious junior colleague and asks "why?" and "what if?" (max 4 questions). 3) Agent notes unclear or wrong parts, student re-explains those. 4) Agent summarizes in 3 lines.
**Persist:** gaps into `weak` in `progress/topics.yaml`; session note.
