# Rapid fire
**When:** warm-up before an interview, or to scan many topics fast.
**Steps:** 1) Pick 8-10 short-answer questions across `focus_topics` (one topic file at a time): definitions, commands, ports, status codes and the like. 2) Ask one by one; student answers in 1-2 sentences. 3) No follow-ups. 4) After: list the ones that were shaky.
**Persist:** shaky ids into `weak`; small score adjustments only.
